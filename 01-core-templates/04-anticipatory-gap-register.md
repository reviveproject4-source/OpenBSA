## 4. Priority guide

- **Critical:** credible severe impact; resolve or explicitly control before proceeding.
- **High:** significant exposure or expensive future change; plan before the relevant milestone.
- **Medium:** meaningful but manageable; schedule or monitor.
- **Low:** limited impact or easily reversible; document and revisit if conditions change.
- **Watch:** plausible but weakly evidenced; define a trigger for reassessment.

Record the reason for each priority. Do not invent numerical probability or financial impact without defensible evidence.
