Governed by the Two-Tier B2E Standard: Specifications are strictly evidence-based and must satisfy the Analysis Integrity Standard (AIS) across 4 dimensions before claiming operational maturity.

Tier 1: Analysis Integrity Standard (AIS)
  • Mathematical & Financial Rigor (Integer Cents)
  • Cross-Blueprint Traceability & Invariant Preserved
  • Empirical Reality Check (Worst-case blast radius)
  • Requirement Integrity & Evidence Traceability
Tier 2: Seven-Pillar Blueprint Standard (7PS)
  • Asumsi & Prasyarat Infrastruktur
  • Failure Scenarios & Blast Radius Model
  • PostgreSQL DDL & Recovery Strategies
  • Security Boundaries & BDD Given-When-Then
